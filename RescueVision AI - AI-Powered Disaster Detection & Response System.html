Data Origin & Verification Transparency
Strict Data Integrity
1. Official Agency Feeds

Directly ingested from verified disaster agencies (USGS, NASA EONET, Open-Meteo). Marked as Official Alert.

2. User Incident Reports

Crowdsourced submissions from the public. Placed in review queue; never marked as verified until authorized coordinator review.

3. Demonstration Alerts

Sample demonstration records created for hackathon evaluation. Always clearly badged as DEMO DATA.

4. Standard Safety Protocols

Standard operating procedures and survival instructions verified by national civil defence authorities.

⚠️

Emergency & AI Limitations Notice

RescueVision AI is an assistive decision-support and emergency information tool. The system does not claim to predict earthquakes or foresee sudden flash disasters without verified sensor networks. Automated image classification requires human verification and must not be used as the sole trigger for emergency response deployment. In an active life-threatening crisis, citizens must always prioritize local civil defence orders and call 112 (India/EU) or 911 immediately.
